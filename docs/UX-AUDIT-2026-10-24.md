# UX audit: Campus Daily vs. the apps students already use (24 Oct 2026)

Method: walked Home, Timetable and Settings at phone (375×812) and desktop (1440×900) widths with sample data
(`/preview`), measured layout, contrast and touch targets in the browser, and compared each screen with the
app students already know for the same job:

| Job in Campus Daily | Benchmark app | Pattern worth copying |
|---|---|---|
| Notices feed | Gmail, Superhuman, Shortwave | Swipe to archive/save, one Undo snackbar, tap opens a detail view, few categories |
| Today / Timetable | Google Calendar, Notion Calendar, Apple Calendar | Current-time line, overlapping events side by side, tap event → detail sheet, swipe between days |
| Deadlines | Todoist, Things "Today" | One "Today" list mixing classes and due items; overdue in red at the top |
| Search / Ask | Notion, Slack, Linear (⌘K), Perplexity | One box that searches everything and answers questions |
| Settings | iOS Settings, Instagram settings | Phone: a list of sections with chevrons, each its own page |
| Whole app | Instagram, WhatsApp | 4–5 bottom tabs with badges, pull to refresh, "updated just now" |

## Already fixed during this audit

- **P0: Home was wider than the phone.** At 375 px the page was 570 px wide: the Today card and Ask box were cut
  off and the Settings tab fell off the bottom bar. Cause: the side rail's grid column grew to fit the scrolling Ask
  suggestions. Fixed in #100 (`.home-rail { grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr) }`), verified scrollWidth = 375.

## Findings, most useful first

### 1. Notices take too long to reach on a phone (Home)
The first notice starts ~900 px down: greeting → nudges → full Today list (5 rows) → Ask box → 8 tabs → search →
note. Google Calendar's widget and Things' Today show *the next thing*, not the whole day.
**Fix:** on phones, show the Today card as one line ("Next: ENT 101 · 17:15 · LH 101 · in 12 min") with
"Full day ›" to expand; drop the section note line; fold Ask into search (see 3). Notices then start above the fold.

### 2. Too many feed tabs, and they scroll sideways
Eight sections (six tabs + Saved + All) in a sideways-scrolling strip; on a phone only 2½ are visible. Gmail uses
at most 5 categories; Shortwave groups into "Important" and "Everything else".
**Fix:** two top-level views. **For you** (Action needed + Exams + My courses, ranked by urgency: overdue, due today,
this week, then course news) and **Explore** (Events, Opportunities, Campus life as filter chips). Saved moves into
the ⋯ menu / a bookmark icon next to search.

### 3. Search and Ask are two separate boxes
Search only filters notices on Home; Ask is a second input above it. Students can't find a Library file, a class
or a setting from one place. Notion/Slack/Linear give one box (⌘K on desktop) for everything.
**Fix:** one "Search or ask" field (app bar icon on phone, ⌘K / `/` on desktop) that searches notices, Library
files and your class-list rows, jumps to settings ("alerts", "division"), and sends questions to Ask.

### 4. Card actions are noisy and undo is inconsistent
Every card shows Done · Save · ⋯; Undo appears inline in different places (cards, timetable, not-interested).
Gmail/Superhuman use swipe gestures plus one bottom snackbar ("Marked done · Undo") for every reversible action.
**Fix:** phone: swipe right = Done, swipe left = Save; one shared snackbar with Undo for Done, Not interested,
Mark cancelled. Desktop: show actions on hover/focus only. Tapping a card opens a detail sheet (full text,
links, add to calendar, source) instead of expanding in place.

### 5. Timetable doesn't behave like a calendar
- Overlapping classes (Thu: CS 101 lecture and the C3 lab) are drawn on top of each other. Google Calendar puts
  them side by side.
- No "now" line, so the current class isn't obvious on the week view.
- On phones the day strip is clipped (Fri hidden) and you can't swipe between days.
- The "Tentative…" note is four lines on every visit.
- Tapping a class does nothing; the only action is cancel.
**Fix:** side-by-side overlap layout, red current-time line, a 5-day strip that fits the width (M T W T F + date)
with swipe, the tentative note as one dismissible line, and a class sheet (time, room with map link, related
notices, Mark cancelled, Edit slot).

### 6. Settings tabs scroll sideways on phones
"You · Courses · Interests · Notifica…" is clipped; Account isn't visible at all. iOS/Instagram settings are a
vertical list of sections, each opening its own page.
**Fix:** on phones, Settings opens as a list (You, Courses, Interests, Notifications, Account, each with a
one-line summary like "D3 · Hostel 16") → tap → section page with a back arrow. Desktop keeps the side rail.

### 7. You can't tell how fresh things are
Nothing says when notices or the timetable last updated, and there's no refresh on the installed app.
**Fix:** "Updated 3 min ago" under the greeting; pull-to-refresh in the installed (standalone) app.

### 8. Bottom tabs carry no signal
**Fix:** small badge on Home when something is due within 48 h, a dot on Timetable when a class today changed.
Consider renaming **Library** to **Files**. Students read "Library" as the Central Library (seats, books).

### 9. Getting the most out of the app depends on install + alerts
Alerts on iPhone only work after "Add to Home Screen", and nothing walks students through it.
**Fix:** one install card (iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen with a picture; Android/Chrome: native install
prompt), shown once after the second visit, then the alerts nudge.

### 10. Keyboard use on laptops
Gmail/Linear power users expect `j`/`k` to move, `e` done, `s` save, `/` search, `g t` timetable, `?` help.
**Fix:** add these on desktop only, listed in a `?` sheet.

### 11. Accessibility details
- "Done" status text on the Today card is 3.1:1 contrast (needs 4.5:1 for small text). Darken it.
- No "Skip to content" link; heading levels jump from h1 to h3 (card titles). Add the link, make section
  headings h2.
- Feed sections use buttons with `aria-pressed`; screen readers expect `role="tablist"`/`tab`.
- Touch targets pass (≥ 34 px; theme toggle is 34×34, raise to 44×44 to match Apple's guideline).
- Check: visible focus ring on cards and the ⋯ menu; `prefers-reduced-motion` for any new swipe/sheet motion.

## What already works well
- The Today timeline with live "Now / In 12 min / Cancelled" states is clearer than most calendar widgets.
- Plain-language copy and short section notes; source icons (mail, WhatsApp, Instagram, Moodle) on cards.
- Colour per course on the week view; dark mode; safe-area padding on the bottom bar.

## Suggested order
1. **Quick wins (≈1 day):** 1 (compact Today on phones), 5 (overlap, now line, day strip, one-line note),
   6 (Settings list on phones), 7 (freshness), 11 (accessibility).
2. **Bigger interaction changes:** 3 (Search or ask), 4 (swipe + snackbar + detail sheet), class sheet from 5.
3. **Structure:** 2 (For you / Explore), 8 (badges, rename), 9 (install card), 10 (shortcuts).
